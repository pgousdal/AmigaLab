# Licensing and export policy

Local preservation and legal redistribution are separate operations. Preserve
everything locally when lawfully possessed. Redistribute nothing without explicit
rights.

Every source has an explicit license profile: `redistributable`, `local-only`,
`commercial`, `personal-backup`, or `unknown`. Media classifications include
open collections, public domain, freeware, shareware, commercial,
licensed-distribution, personal-backup, and unknown. Unknown is the default;
downloadability or local ownership does not imply redistribution rights.

M2.3 does not implement public export. Commercial, licensed-distribution,
local-only, personal-backup, and unknown content is non-redistributable by
default. Amiga Forever images are valid local sources, but no such media is
included in this repository or copied to redistributable collections.
